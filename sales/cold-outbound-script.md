# CazoTask — Cold Outbound Sales Script

Target buyer: owners/operators of small-to-mid-size businesses (10-200 employees) who run manual, repetitive processes — agency founders, ops managers, sales directors, ecommerce owners, professional services firms (law, accounting, real estate, insurance).

Pain hook: they're losing time/money to manual lead follow-up, data entry, and cross-tool busywork.

---

## 1. Cold Call Script

**Opening (first 10 seconds — earn the next 20):**

> "Hi [Name], this is [Your Name] with CazoTask — I'll be quick, this is a cold call. I work with [their industry] businesses to cut out the manual busywork that eats up their team's day. Got 30 seconds?"

**If yes — Pain-first pivot:**

> "Quick question — when a new lead comes in, or when data has to move between your CRM and [spreadsheet/invoicing/scheduling tool], is that still mostly a person doing it by hand?"

*(Pause. Let them answer. Listen for pain: slow follow-up, missed leads, double data entry, hiring for repetitive roles.)*

**Value statement:**

> "That's exactly what we fix. We build AI automations that plug into the tools you already use — so leads get an instant response, data moves itself, and your team only touches the stuff that actually needs a human. Most clients get 10-20 hours a week back within the first month."

**Ask for the meeting:**

> "I'd rather show you than explain it — I do a free 15-minute process audit where I map exactly where you're bleeding time and what it'd take to fix it, no pitch, no obligation. Does Tuesday or Thursday afternoon work better for a quick call?"

**Common objections:**

- *"We already have a CRM/tool for that."* → "Good — we don't replace your stack, we plug into it. This is about automating what your team still does by hand around it."
- *"Not interested / too busy."* → "Totally fair — busy is exactly the problem I solve. I'll send a 60-second video showing what this looks like for a business like yours, no pressure to respond."
- *"How much does this cost?"* → "It depends on the workflow, but most engagements pay for themselves in the first month from time saved. That's exactly what the free audit tells us before you spend a dollar."
- *"Send me an email."* → "Happy to — what's the best address? I'll keep it short."

**Close (voicemail version, 20 seconds):**

> "Hi [Name], [Your Name] with CazoTask. We help [industry] businesses automate the manual busywork — lead follow-up, data entry, that kind of thing — using AI. I'll follow up with a quick email, but if you want to skip ahead, call me back at [number]. Thanks!"

---

## 2. Discovery Call Script (once they say yes)

1. **Open:** Thank them, confirm time (15-20 min), restate the goal: "identify where you're losing the most hours to manual work, and whether automation makes sense."
2. **Discovery questions:**
   - "Walk me through what happens from the moment a new lead/customer comes in to when they're fully onboarded."
   - "Which of those steps are done manually today, and who does them?"
   - "What tools are you using — CRM, spreadsheets, email, scheduling, invoicing?"
   - "If you got 10 hours a week back on your team, what would you do with it?"
   - "Have you tried automating any of this before? What happened?"
3. **Diagnose + reflect:** Summarize back the 1-2 biggest time sinks you heard.
4. **Propose next step:** "Based on what you've told me, I think [specific workflow] is the highest-impact place to start. I'll put together a short proposal with scope and pricing — can I send that over by [day]?"
5. **Close the loop:** Confirm decision-maker, timeline, and budget range before ending the call.

---

## 3. Qualifying Criteria (who to prioritize)

- Company has repetitive manual workflows (lead follow-up, data entry, scheduling, reporting)
- 10+ employees or growing fast enough that hiring is the current "solution" to workload
- Already using digital tools (CRM, spreadsheets, email) — automation has something to plug into
- A named decision-maker is reachable directly (owner, ops lead, sales director)
