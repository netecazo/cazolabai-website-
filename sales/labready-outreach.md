# LabReady Pro — Outreach Templates

For lab managers, chemistry supervisors, lab directors and quality managers. Personalise every [bracketed] field. Warm contacts first, cold second. Only email people with a plausible professional reason to hear from you, include your real name and a physical mailing address, and honour opt-outs immediately (CAN-SPAM).

---

> Hi [First Name],
>
> After all these years at the bench, I've finally started building the thing I always wished we had: a proper system for chemistry training and competency.
>

>
> I'm looking for two or three chemistry departments to pilot it free for 60 days. Would yours be a fit, or is there someone you'd point me to?
>

>
> Elie

## Discovery call questions (20 minutes)

1. How do you run annual and new-hire competency today? Paper, spreadsheet, LMS, CAP Hub?
2. Roughly how many hours a year do you or your leads spend on it?
3. When's your next inspection? What worried you last time?
4. How do new techs learn QC troubleshooting now?
5. Which analysers do you run?
6. If this saved you [X] hours and made competency records inspection-ready, who would approve a ~$3,000/year licence, and when is budget set?
7. Would you try it free for 60 days and give me feedback every couple of weeks?

## Demo flow (30 minutes)

1. Their pain back to them in one sentence (from discovery)
2. Supervisor dashboard with their test systems and a sample staff list
3. One competency package end to end: assign → tech completes → supervisor signs → PDF record
4. QC Troubleshooting Assistant on the "Level 3 out, Level 1 in" case
5. Pilot terms and next step booked before hanging up
