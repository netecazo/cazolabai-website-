## Compliance note (read before sending any of these)

- **CAN-SPAM (US):** cold B2B email is legal but requires: accurate sender info, a physical mailing address in the footer, no deceptive subject lines, and a working unsubscribe/opt-out honored within 10 business days.
- **GDPR/UK-GDPR / CASL (Canada):** if contacting people in the EU/UK/Canada, you generally need a lawful basis (e.g. legitimate interest for B2B outreach) and must honor opt-out immediately; CASL requires consent or an existing business relationship for Canadian recipients.
- Only email people/roles with a plausible business reason to hear from you (their job function relates to what you're selling), keep a suppression list of opt-outs, and stop immediately on request.
- I did not source or scrape a live prospect list for you — see the note in the main reply about why, and how to build one properly (Apollo.io, LinkedIn Sales Navigator, ZoomInfo, or your own network) before sending these.
